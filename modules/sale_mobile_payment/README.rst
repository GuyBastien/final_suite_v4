Sale Mobile Payment Module
##########################

Pay a customer invoice, or a sale through its invoice, with MonCash,
NatCash or any operator added to mobile_payment. The payment is booked with
the standard invoice payment (reconciliation), on the *Invoice Payment Method*
chosen on the mobile payment account. Dialogue, QR code, scheduler and
notifications are those of mobile_payment.
