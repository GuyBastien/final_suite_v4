Fuel Station Cash Control
#########################

Banknote and coin count of the shifts of ``fuel_station``. It reuses the
denominations of ``sale_point_cash_control`` (Sales > Denominations), so the
same banknotes and coins serve the shops, the points of sale and the
fuel stations. The count supports suspect (counterfeit) banknotes and coins
and warns when the counted cash differs from the cash payments.
